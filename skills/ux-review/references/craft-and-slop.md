# Surface modes, craft floor, and anti-slop discipline

Use this reference to calibrate visual ambition to the visitor's job, enforce the baseline craft floor before shipping UI, detect generic AI-generated ("slop") patterns, and steer targeted design refinements. Read alongside [interface-design.md](interface-design.md) and [visual-review.md](visual-review.md).

## Choose the surface mode from the visitor's job

Select the mode per surface or route, never for the entire company. A developer tool's dashboard is **Operate**, its documentation is **Read**, its homepage is **Persuade**, and its interactive playground showcase is **Experience**.

| Mode | Visitor's success | Composition & hierarchy | Typography, color & motion register | Cross-mode failure to reject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Operate** (app UI, dashboards, tables, editors, admin, settings) | Complete a task accurately and repeatedly. | Earned familiarity, spatial stability, predictable controls, high working-area ratio. | Single workhorse UI family, fixed `rem` role scale (1.125–1.2 ratio), restrained neutrals + semantic state colors, fast state-only transitions (100–250 ms), zero page-load choreography. | Injecting marketing hero banners, oversized fluid headings, decorative glows, or invented controls into a working tool. |
| **Read** (docs, guides, API references, articles, changelogs) | Find, read, and understand an answer. | Calm reading column (60–75ch prose measure) inside an unmistakable wayfinding frame. | Comfortable body floor (≥16px web prose), clear heading rhythm, monospace reserved for code/parameters/measurements; brand identity owns the frame (masthead, rail, callouts) without invading the reading column. | Turning documentation into a poster or marketing card grid, or letting unconstrained full-bleed lines stretch past 80ch. |
| **Persuade** (landing pages, product launches, pricing, campaigns) | Understand the distinct value, trust the proof, and take a concrete action. | Point-of-view composition, unmistakable first-viewport focal anchor, varied section cadence (peaks, asymmetric splits, comparative proof, breathing room). | Decisive display-to-body contrast, page-scale tonal/color commitment, one authored focal interaction or transition rather than identical scroll fades on every section. | Assembling a timid admin-style grid of identical 3-up icon cards, vague superlative copy, and generic abstract placeholders. |
| **Experience** (interactive showcases, portfolios, galleries, live demos) | Engage directly with the work or artifact itself. | The live artifact leads from the first viewport; surrounding chrome recedes. | Framing, lighting, and transitions serve the artifact's material and responsiveness without competing chrome. | Burying the interactive work below walls of explanatory chrome or static screenshots of an experience that could run live. |

## Persuade and showcase architecture

When designing or reviewing a **Persuade** surface, evaluate whether the page earns belief and action through structure rather than decoration:

1. **First-viewport conversion contract:** Within seconds, a domain-competent visitor must see (a) what the product concretely does and why it differs, (b) a visible, honest primary action in working form (plus at most one secondary path), and (c) an authentic product proof or signature artifact that only this product could demonstrate (for example, a live or realistic routing/workflow trace, interactive comparison, executable command, or real interface state — never a decorative pile of floating abstract cards). Balance multi-column openings so one column does not stretch far past the fold while its sibling floats in dead space.
2. **Varied section rhythm and the skeleton test:** Strip the text out and inspect the bare spatial skeleton. Every major section should have a distinct structural job — an asymmetric product walkthrough, a measured comparative proof, a compact technical integration strip, a decision table, a focused closing action — rather than repeating the same centered-heading-over-three-equal-cards scaffold down the scroll.
3. **Proof before superlative:** Replace generic claims with verifiable mechanisms, concrete workflows, realistic units, and honest methodology links. Commercial facts, benchmarks, pricing, customer names, and capabilities must come strictly from authoritative truth; label illustrative sample data honestly when used.

## Baseline craft floor

Verify these mechanics on the rendered output before considering any UI complete:

- **Typography and line breaks:** Maintain unmistakable separation between display, section heading, body, label, and metadata roles. Keep prose lines within 60–75ch and multi-line leading ≥1.5 (never <1.3). Set `text-wrap: balance` on multi-line headings (ensuring full-width borders live on containers rather than balanced inline boxes) and `text-wrap: pretty` on prose where supported. Apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to metrics, counters, prices, timestamps, and table columns. Keep interactive/functional UI text ≥11px and ordinary body prose ≥14–16px. Tighten display tracking optically (never tighter than `-0.04em`; keep body tracking near normal). On dark surfaces, compensate light text optically with slightly more leading or weight so characters do not halate or thin out. In multi-script interfaces (such as CJK paired with Latin/numerals), verify baseline alignment, line-height comfort, and mixed-script wrapping at every target width.
- **Spacing and heading rhythm:** Group related items tightly and separate distinct sections generously instead of repeating one uniform gap everywhere. A heading binds to the content it introduces: rendered space above a heading must exceed the space below it (except the first heading inside a bounded container). Keep at least 12–16px of internal padding inside bordered or tinted containers and ≥16–24px horizontal viewport gutters on narrow screens.
- **Color, contrast, and composed themes:** Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (≥4.5:1 normal text and placeholders, ≥3:1 large text, icons, boundaries, and focus indicators) in computed pixels across themes. Never place neutral gray text over a saturated or tinted surface — derive secondary text from the foreground or surface hue so it stays crisp rather than muddy. Compose dark mode explicitly through intentional surface elevation steps and reduced extreme chroma rather than mechanically inverting light mode. Declare container elevation once: choose either a crisp hairline border or a soft offset shadow for repeated surfaces, reserving combined border-plus-shadow for genuinely floating overlays, menus, and dialogs.
- **Motion and liveness:** Keep all content visible at rest (`opacity: 1`) so delayed or failed scripts never leave blank sections. Favor natural exponential deceleration (such as `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), with exits faster than entrances; avoid bounce or elastic curves by reflex. Provide an intentional `prefers-reduced-motion` path that removes spatial travel and nonessential loops while preserving opacity/color state feedback.
- **Unstyled browser surfaces:** Theme `::selection`, `:focus-visible` rings, caret color, underline offset, scrollbars, and metric-compatible font fallbacks from the design system so browser defaults do not betray an otherwise cohesive surface.

## Design specificity and anti-slop catalog

Before running automated measurements, perform the **Design Specificity Check**: cover the brand mark and ask whether an unrelated product in the same category could ship this layout and visual language unchanged. Treat the patterns below as high-signal diagnostic triggers, always checking the legitimate counterexample boundary before flagging a defect:

| Category | Observable tell | Failure mechanism | Legitimate boundary / counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Layout** | Monotonous 3-up card grids (`icon tile + title + blurb` repeated section after section) | Flattens distinct arguments into equal-weight boxes; forces scanning overhead without hierarchy. | Genuinely comparable peers the user chooses among (for example, pricing tiers, model/template catalog items, or status tiles). |
| **Layout** | Rounded-square tinted icon tile stacked above every heading | Recognizable generator template that consumes vertical space without adding meaning. | Distinct status/object emblems where iconography encodes a real type distinction, or compact inline icon-beside-label pairs. |
| **Layout** | Nested cards (bordered/tinted cards inside bordered cards) and "ghost cards" (1px border + wide diffuse shadow on every grid cell) | Creates visual clutter, double gutters, and ambiguous elevation hierarchy. | One genuinely floating interactive preview, dialog, or popover staged over a canvas surface. |
| **Type & structure** | Kicker / eyebrow pill or tracked uppercase micro-label stacked above every section heading, or `01 / 02 / 03` chapter numbers | Redundant editorial scaffolding that weakens the heading's own voice. | A true breadcrumb/category scope indicator, or an actual ordered procedure where step sequence carries operational meaning. |
| **Type & copy** | Monospace used as a "technical" costume for headings or body prose | Degrades reading speed and turns semantic code typography into decoration. | Actual code, CLI commands, API paths, identifiers, keybindings, or aligned numeric telemetry. |
| **Type & copy** | Vague SaaS buzzwords (*supercharge, seamless, empower, next-gen, world-class*) or repeated aphoristic cadence (*"Not X. Just Y."* across multiple sections) | Replaces concrete product comprehension with interchangeable filler cadence. | Plain, specific verbs and nouns naming the actual actor, mechanism, constraint, and measurable outcome. |
| **Color & depth** | Gradient-filled text (`background-clip: text`), zero-offset neon/colored glow shadows, purple-to-cyan AI gradients, or floating radial spotlight hazes | Borrows synthetic "AI aesthetic" clichés that reduce contrast and age poorly. | Intentional, user-pinned brand artwork or a real optical/chart scale encoding continuous data values. |
| **Color & depth** | Thick colored `border-left` / `border-right` stripe (>1px) on rounded cards | Clashes geometrically with rounded corners and screams generated callout template. | Square-cornered code diff gutters, quotation bars, timeline tracks, or dense log severity rails. |
| **Motion & SVG** | Pulsing status dots on static marketing labels, fake blinking terminal cursors on non-inputs, auto-scrolling marquees, or hero clip-art built from primitive SVG shapes | Simulates fake liveness, hides half the marquee content, or cheapens the focal visual. | Live telemetry indicators tied to real polling/streaming state, actual focusable inputs, or crisp geometric diagrams/schematics with exact technical meaning. |

## Cognitive load and working-memory probes

Distinguish three kinds of mental effort during walkthroughs:

- **Intrinsic load** (inherent domain or decision complexity): structure it with sensible defaults, clear units, co-located decision data, and step sequencing; do not oversimplify away controls experts actually need.
- **Extraneous load** (design-inflicted waste): eliminate visual noise, unexplained internal jargon, inconsistent action patterns, and "memory bridges" that force users to remember values from a previous screen or tab.
- **Germane load** (effort that builds transferable mental models): support it with consistent object lifecycles and immediate, truthful feedback.

At any single decision point, count the distinct ungrouped options or actions competing simultaneously (**working-memory check**, ~4 chunks per Cowan, 2001). When 5–7+ ungrouped choices compete in a primary action bar, top navigation, or hero CTA cluster, establish one primary action, 1–2 secondary paths, and group or progressively disclose the rest. *Counterexample:* dense comparison tables, filterable catalogs, and reference indexes exist specifically for scanning many peers; judge those by sort/filter clarity and column scanability rather than capping visible rows at four.

## Design steering and finishing vocabulary

Translate review findings or user direction into concrete, scoped design moves rather than vague aesthetic adjectives:

| Move | Diagnostic trigger | Concrete execution discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`distill`** | Too many containers, competing buttons, repeated explanations, or decorative clutter. | Strip to one primary goal per region; remove redundant wrappers, duplicate intros, and unnecessary cards; let proximity and alignment carry structure. |
| **`bolder`** | A `Persuade` or showcase section reads timid, flat, or indistinguishable from neighbors. | Amplify using the product's own strongest type scale, tonal contrast, and real artifact; commit to one decisive focal peak and quiet surrounding elements so it lands. Do not pile on random effects. |
| **`quieter`** | Visual fatigue from oversaturated colors, competing heavy weights, borders, or motion. | Reduce saturation and border noise, step down competing bold weights, widen whitespace, and calm transitions while keeping a distinct point of view (restraint is not washed-out gray). |
| **`layout`** | Weak squint-test reading order, uniform gaps, cramped padding, or awkward responsive reflow. | State the spatial thesis first; group by proximity before borders; contrast tight intra-group spacing with generous section breaks; adapt structure (not just scale) across wide, intermediate, and narrow widths. |
| **`typeset`** | Flat heading hierarchy, long unreadable lines, ragged single-word tails, or misaligned numbers. | Sharpen role scale and weight steps, constrain prose to 60–75ch, apply `balance`/`pretty` and `tabular-nums`, verify mixed-script rhythm and 200% zoom. |
| **`colorize`** | Monochromatic flatness that hides action/state, or scattered uncoordinated accents. | Assign clear surface, text, action, and semantic roles; let committed color own deliberate regions or states; verify computed AA contrast in both light and dark themes. |
| **`animate`** | Abrupt spatial jumps, missing state continuity, or repetitive scroll-reveal clutter. | Remove generic section fades; author only transitions that clarify state/spatial continuity or one product-specific focal sequence, with exponential ease-out and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. |
| **`clarify`** | Internal jargon, vague CTAs, cryptic errors, or redundant helper text. | Say each idea once in the user's domain language; make actions verb+object specific; ensure every error states what failed, why (when known), and how to recover. |
| **`harden` & `adapt`** | Fragile layouts under real data, translation expansion, touch input, or network failures. | Enforce `min-width: 0` in flex/grid children, 30–40% i18n expansion budget, CJK/RTL safety, `@media (hover: hover)` vs coarse touch targets (≥24×24 CSS px AA floor, 44×44 touch comfort), interrupted gesture cleanup (`pointercancel`/`lostpointercapture`), and honest empty/offline/error recovery. |
| **`polish`** | Final pre-delivery pass within an established design direction. | Fix optical alignment, design-system drift, browser surfaces, console/asset errors, keyboard focus order, and dead code without smuggling in an unauthorized redesign. |
